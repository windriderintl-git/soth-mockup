All Are Welcome ELCA Lutheran Edina, MN

A church where you can show up as yourself.

We love God and love neighbor in the spirit of Jesus. LGBTQ+ people, lifelong Lutherans, skeptics, young families, and elders all belong here, in worship, friendship, learning, and service. Come as you are. We mean it.

Our welcome

All are welcome. No exceptions.

We are a place where all people can receive wholeness and share God's blessings, so we echo Jesus' welcome to all persons, everyone:

The devout, the seeker, and the skeptic Single, married, widowed, and divorced Young and old, child and parent All sexual orientations and gender identities All lands, cultures, and colors People with disabilities, those ill and well Poor, middling, and wealthy All who seek hope, peace, comfort, and grace

Meet our pastor

Rev. Dr. Yolanda Denson-Byers (she/her)

Pastor Yolanda has served in parish, hospice, hospital, prison, and campus ministry for more than 25 years. She holds an M.Div. from Harvard and a D.Min. from Luther Seminary, and is the author of See Me, Believe Me.

"You don't have to have faith figured out to belong here. Bring your questions, your doubts, and your whole self — that's exactly who Jesus welcomed."

For families

Kids aren't an interruption here. They're the church too.
