Who we are

Small enough to know your name. Big enough to matter.

Shepherd of the Hills is a neighborhood ELCA congregation on the Edina–Hopkins border. Our mission is one sentence: to love God and love neighbor in the spirit of Jesus. Here are the people who help that happen.

Senior Pastor

Rev. Dr. Yolanda Denson-Byers (she/her)

Pastor Yolanda began her call at SOTH in July 2023, bringing more than 25 years of ordained ministry across parish, hospice, hospital, prison, and campus settings.

She is a public theologian and activist, holds a B.A. from Wesleyan University, an M.Div. from Harvard, and a D.Min. from Luther Seminary, and is the author of See Me, Believe Me. Most Sundays you'll find her at the door afterward, and she'd genuinely like to meet you.

pastoryolanda@sothchurch.com

Our place

500 Blake Road South

A welcoming, accessible building on the Edina–Hopkins border: sanctuary, fellowship spaces, Ranum Hall (home of the quilters), and a lawn made for campfires.
