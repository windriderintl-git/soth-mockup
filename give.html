Give

Generosity grows from gratitude, and it stays in the neighborhood.

We give because God first gave to us, not because anyone is keeping score. Every dollar given here funds worship, kids and youth, care for our members, and more than twenty community partnerships within a few miles of our front door.

Give securely online

Online giving is processed by Vanco, our secure payment partner. One-time and recurring gifts available.

Where it goes

What your generosity actually does.

Worship & music

Sunday worship, two music directors across traditions, and the AV upgrade making worship easier to join from anywhere.

Kids & youth

PrayGround, Sunday School, confirmation, youth group, camp scholarships, and VBS.

Care

Home and hospital visits, funeral care, Mental Health Connect, and a pastor who shows up.

Neighbors

Food shelves, housing partners, quilts for shelters, and the Onward young-adult housing partnership.

Ways to give

However works for you.

Online

One-time or recurring gifts by card or bank transfer through Vanco. Recurring gifts are the steadiest way to support the ministry.

Give online

In worship or by mail

The offering plate works just fine, or mail a check to Shepherd of the Hills Lutheran Church, 500 Blake Road South, Edina, MN 55343.

Stock, IRA & legacy gifts

Gifts of securities, IRA qualified charitable distributions, and bequests are all possible. Contact the office and our financial secretary will walk you through it.

Our promise about money

The budget is approved by the congregation, reported openly, and overseen by elected lay leaders. Questions are welcome; ask Treasurer Jodi Christiansen or the office anytime.

Ask a giving question

Visitors: please don't feel obligated.

If you're new, your presence is the gift. Giving is for people who've decided this community is theirs to sustain.
