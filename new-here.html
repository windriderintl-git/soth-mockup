First time here?

Your first Sunday, without the guesswork.

Walking into a new church can feel intimidating, so here is everything, honestly: where to park, what to wear, what happens in worship, and what's there for your kids. Come once, sit anywhere, and leave whenever you need to.

How a first visit goes

Four steps. None of them scary.

Before you come

Optional: send a visit note below and a greeter will watch for you at the door. Or just show up; that's how most people start.

When you arrive

Park in the church lot, come through the main doors, and a greeter will point you anywhere you need: seats, restrooms, nursery, coffee.

During worship

About an hour: music, scripture, a children's message, honest preaching, prayer, and open communion. Everything is printed or on screen. No secret handshake.

After worship

Stay for coffee hour at 10:30 if you'd like to meet people. Slipping out quietly is also completely fine. Come back when you're ready.

What to expect

The honest details.

Parking & arrival

Free parking in the church lot, with accessible spaces near the entrance. Enter through the main doors, where greeters will meet you.

What to wear

Anything. You'll see jeans, casual clothes, and Sunday best. We call it a "no shame zone." Wear what lets you be present.

Communion

Everyone is welcome at the table, no membership required. Bread, wine, grape juice, and gluten-free wafers are available, and the pastor will bring communion to your seat on request.

Kids in worship

Kids belong in worship here. There's a children's message, a PrayGround for little ones, worship activity bags, and a nursery if you need it.

Accessibility

Accessible parking and entry, space for wheelchairs in the sanctuary, and worship on Zoom if getting here is hard. Tell us what would help and we'll make it work.

No pressure, ever

You won't be asked to stand up, introduce yourself, or give money. There's a welcome desk with a small gift if you want to say hi, and that's it.

Plan Your Visit

Tell us when you might come and a greeter will meet you at the door. No follow-up barrage, we promise.

Questions people actually ask

First-visit FAQ

How long is the service, really?

About an hour. Worship starts at 9:30 and most Sundays we're done shortly after 10:30. Coffee hour afterward is optional and as long as you want it to be.

I'm LGBTQ+. Am I actually welcome, or just tolerated?

Welcome, fully. Our welcome statement names people of all sexual orientations and gender identities on purpose, and it covers every part of church life: worship, communion, baptism, leadership, marriage, and service. You won't be an exception here. Read more on our Welcome & Beliefs page.

I haven't been to church in years (or ever). Will I be lost?

No. Everything is printed in the bulletin or on screen, and nobody expects you to know when to stand or what to say. Plenty of people here came back to church after a long time away, or arrived skeptical. Both are honored ways to show up.

Do my kids have to sit still the whole time?

Definitely not. Young children can worship from the PrayGround, grab a worship activity bag, or use the nursery. There's a children's message in every service, Sunday School on first Sundays, and nobody glares at wiggly kids here.

Will I be asked for money?

An offering happens during worship because generosity is part of our practice, but you're our guest. Let the plate pass by with zero awkwardness.

Do I have to believe everything to participate?

No. We believe love, not doctrine, is at the center of it all. Seekers and skeptics are explicitly welcome. Bring your questions.

Can I check it out online first?

Yes. Worship happens live on Zoom every Sunday at 9:30, and recordings are on our YouTube channel. Lurking is allowed.

Is the building accessible?

Yes: accessible parking and entry, plus room in the sanctuary for wheelchairs and mobility devices. If you have a specific need, call ahead at (952) 935-3457 and we'll have things ready.

Can't make it in person? Worship from your couch.

Every Sunday at 9:30 AM, our service runs live on Zoom, and real people will be glad you're there, even through a screen.
